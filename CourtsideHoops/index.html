About Courtside Hoops

The premium digital home for NBA card collectors

🏀 Our Story

Courtside Hoops was built by collectors, for collectors. Born out of frustration with fragmented marketplaces, we set out to create one unified, beautiful platform where NBA card enthusiasts can browse, buy, and trade the cards they love.

🎯 Our Mission

To deliver a reliable, engaging, and user-friendly marketplace for rare and collectible NBA trading cards — connecting fans, streamlining transactions, and celebrating the culture of the game.

💎 Our Promise

Every card listed on our platform is verified for authenticity. We prioritize quality, transparency, and collector satisfaction above all else.

🚀 The Future

We're working on real-time inventory, a loyalty points system, and expanded card detail pages with live player statistics. Courtside Hoops is just getting started.
